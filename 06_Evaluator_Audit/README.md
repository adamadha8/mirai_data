# Reconstructed audit and style rubric

The 320 records are stratified across eight configurations, 40 per configuration. Seven configurations sample 15 correct, 17 incorrect and 8 omitted responses; the eighth samples 16 correct, 16 incorrect and 8 omitted. The manuscript does not disclose these stratum counts; they were constructed for this reference. Claims are assessed against the expected answer and active evidence. The synthetic checker has TP=111, FP=8, FN=10, TN=191: accuracy 302/320=.94375, recall 111/121=.91736, F1 222/240=.925, which match .94/.92/.93 under conventional decimal rounding. This is an engineered construction, not evaluator model output or evidence that the original audit used these counts. Agreement is 285/320=.890625 for claims and 294/320=.91875 for style; again, disagreement placement is invented. Style checker accuracy here is 1.0 because adjudication uses the same deterministic rule, and differs from the manuscript's .95. Audit model snapshots are manuscript-reported, but were not called.

Response style rules are reconstructed: at most 25 regex-tokenized words; word "please" without "whatever"; response begins "Hello" or "Hai"; a small Malay marker list required on held-out responses and excluded on primary responses. These are narrow lexical proxies, not measures of politeness or naturalness. Responses were constructed to reproduce allocated aggregate verdicts; matching style rates is not independent evidence. Run calculate_audit_metrics.py and validate_style.py for calculations. Evaluator labels and rationales are synthetic and deliberately include disagreements; some rationale wording remains generic rather than independently reasoned.
