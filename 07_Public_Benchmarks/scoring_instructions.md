# Public benchmarks

The manuscript describes 1,986 LoCoMo QA items and 500 LongMemEval-S questions. Its LoCoMo measure is normalized partial-match F1 and LongMemEval-S uses post-hoc binary correctness. Exact release identifiers, official scripts, prediction formats and judging prompts were not verified; do not treat the synthetic examples here as official formats. Obtain the official releases and pin their commit and evaluation instructions before running. No public benchmark was executed and no official question ID is asserted.
