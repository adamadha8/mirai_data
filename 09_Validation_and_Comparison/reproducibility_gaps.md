# Remaining gaps and limits

The dialogue is generated from templates. Exact duplicates are substantially reduced, but prose is sometimes mechanical and some response attributes remain weakly contextualized. Realistic extended multi-turn discussions, uncertainty, and full consent scenarios are not established by the duplicate test. Item outcomes are assigned to match aggregate results, not produced by the local implementation. Responses are mechanically constructed to make the four narrow style checks reflect the assigned counts; the checks are not natural-language judgments. Evaluator judgments and disagreements are reconstructed; some rationales are generic. Actual original records and independent evaluator outputs remain unavailable.

The eight configurations are executable simplified teaching examples, using manually assigned retrieval scores and deterministic values, not actual embeddings, model prompts or full implementations. Shared examples do not establish that every ablation has a distinct final-answer result. Tables 3, 5 and 6 have comparisons; targeted Table 4 bootstrap intervals, Table 7 and 9 transcriptions appear in additional_results.csv. The full inventory of every numerical mention, including pooled item bootstrap and all textual contrasts, remains incomplete. No real benchmark predictions or official benchmark scoring were run; integration covers structural inspection only, and public scoring needs official dependencies, datasets and (for LongMemEval) a judge model. Synthetic timing logs are not measured experiment timings.

Safe derived-output rebuild: from the folder run `python rebuild.py --output <new-absolute-directory>`. It refuses to overwrite existing destinations, copies the existing reference dataset and documentation, then rebuilds selected derived files and executes validation there. It does not regenerate underlying records from first principles; old generation and complete_package scripts are legacy and can overwrite revised data/documentation. Rebuild was tested in a separate temporary directory. Use the original manuscript and real logs to reproduce the original experiment.
