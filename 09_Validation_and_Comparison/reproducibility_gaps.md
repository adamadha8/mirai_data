# Reproducibility gaps

This package is a partial reference reconstruction. It does not supply the original persona fields, prompt text, model responses, evidence retrieval scores, checker outputs, audit rationales, response timings, or public benchmark predictions. The synthetic content is schematic and repetitive, not a realistic recovery of natural dialogue. The source-attribution trials do not yet include independent third-party distractor records; the audit style verdicts, meaningful strata, and documented evaluator decision rubrics are not reconstructed. The local demonstration does not implement all baselines, source authority updates, semantic embeddings, recipient style, claim extraction, rewrite, or full ablation behavior. It should not be used to claim an implementation replication.

The comparison CSV covers Tables 3 and 5; Tables 4, 6, 7 and 9 are not comprehensively compared observation-by-observation. Held-out F1 is approximate. Bootstrap and randomization seeds are assumptions; reconstructed confidence intervals and p-values cannot verify Table 6. Audit labels were generated mechanically and do not reproduce reported audit accuracy or agreement. No public dataset or official scoring version was verified or run. Aggregate timings are transcribed but no fabricated per-response timings are asserted.
