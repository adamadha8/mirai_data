Dialogue: 4488 turns, 4464 unique exact texts, 24 exact duplicates; most repeated text occurs 2 times. Text is template-generated; exact diversity does not establish naturalness.

Style verdicts are checked against constructed response text with a limited deterministic rule set. Configuration demos are simplified, and attribution errors are scenario-based allocations rather than outputs of those demos. This validation cannot establish original-study reproducibility.
